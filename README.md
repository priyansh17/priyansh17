<h4> About Me </h4>

I’m a Software Engineer at Microsoft, currently working on Azure BCDR (Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery) ensuring that customers' workloads stay resilient and recoverable in the face of disruptions. My work focuses on building and maintaining cloud-native solutions that are scalable, extensible, and operationally efficient.

Previously at Cloudera, I was part of the Cloud Infrastructure team where I orchestrated Kubernetes and Docker environments and managed large-scale clusters using tools like Ansible and CI/CD pipelines. This role gave me a strong foundation in DevOps and internal infrastructure automation.

I’m also exploring the open-source world, contributing to projects like Velero and Apache YuniKorn, and continuously growing in the cloud-native ecosystem. My technical toolkit includes C/C++, Golang, Python, JavaScript, and C#, along with deep experience in Kubernetes and AKS.
